For Ontario businesses that rent 5 to 40 locations: clinic groups, franchisees and multi-site retailers.

One missed date can cost you the location.

Most commercial leases let you renew only if written notice reaches the landlord by a set date, often 6 to 12 months before the lease ends.

Miss it and the landlord can refuse to renew, or ask for a new and higher rent. Ontario courts hold tenants to the exact date. In a 2025 case, a restaurant that had put about $100,000 into its premises missed an eight-month notice deadline and had to go to court to keep its location (case summary).

With many locations, these dates sit in different PDFs, a spreadsheet or one person's memory. That is how they get missed.

Questions

Why not lease software?

Software is an empty tool. You must type the dates, and you must notice the reminder. We do the reading, a person checks every date against the lease, and we phone you if a renewal deadline is close and nobody has confirmed it.

Who checks the facts?

A person reviews every term against the lease before it is approved. Software helps find the clauses. It never approves anything on its own.

Where are my documents kept?

In a private folder used only for your company, with regular backups. We do not sell or share your documents. We use an AI reading tool only if you agree in writing, and only through providers that keep no copy of your lease. A person still checks every fact.

What if my leases are scanned or old?

Scanned copies are fine. If a page cannot be read clearly, we tell you and ask for a better copy. We never guess.

Do I need to install anything?

No. You get a calendar you add to Outlook or Google Calendar, a portfolio report, a PDF summary per location and a weekly email.

Is this legal advice?

No. We record what your lease says and when things are due. For decisions about renewing or negotiating, talk to your lawyer or broker.
