Questions

Why not lease software?

Software is an empty tool. You must find and type the dates, and a wrong date looks the same as a right one. We do the reading, and a person checks every date against the lease. Software for tenants costs less; it does not do that work.

Do you use AI?

Yes, for the first draft, on every lease. It runs only through providers that keep no copy of your document and do not train on it. A person then checks every fact against the lease page before it is approved. If you need reading with no AI, ask us: it costs $200 more per location.

Where are my documents kept?

In a private folder used only for your company, with regular backups. We do not sell or share your documents, and we delete them when you ask.

What if my leases are scanned or old?

Scanned copies are fine. If a page cannot be read clearly, we tell you and ask for a better copy. We never guess.

Do I need to install anything?

No. You get a calendar you add to Outlook or Google Calendar, a portfolio report, a PDF summary per location and a weekly email.

Is this legal advice?

No. We record what your lease says and when things are due. For decisions about renewing or negotiating, talk to your lawyer or broker.
