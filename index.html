Why it matters

Renewal options are strict. Most commercial leases give the tenant the right to renew only if written notice reaches the landlord by a set date — often 6 to 12 months before the lease ends. Miss that date and the landlord can refuse to renew or ask for a new, higher rent. When those dates live in a spreadsheet or one person's memory, they get missed.

Questions

Who checks the facts?

A person reviews every term against the lease before it is approved. Software helps find the clauses; it never approves anything on its own.

Where are my documents kept?

On an encrypted computer, in a folder used only for your company. We do not sell or share your documents, and they are never used to train AI.

Is this legal advice?

No. We record what your lease says and when things are due. For decisions about renewing or negotiating, talk to your lawyer or broker.
